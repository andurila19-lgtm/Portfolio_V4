## 📋 Project Overview (Ringkasan Proyek)

**SIM Akademik** is a comprehensive Academic Management Information System designed for higher education institutions. It provides tailored dashboards and specialized access control for Super Admins, Lecturers, Academic Staff, and Finance Personnel.

*SIM Akademik adalah Sistem Informasi Manajemen Akademik perguruan tinggi yang dirancang untuk mengelola seluruh operasional akademik, data perkuliahan, hingga keuangan dalam satu platform terintegrasi berbasis multi-role.*

## 🔍 Features (Fitur Utama)

- 👑 **Super Admin Suite:** Full system configuration, user management, and audit logs. (*Manajemen pengguna dan konfigurasi sistem utama.*)
- 👨‍🏫 **Portal Dosen:** Grading, class attendance management, and student advice. (*Input nilai, presensi perkuliahan, dan bimbingan akademik.*)
- 🏫 **Staff Akademik:** Course scheduling, curriculum management, and student enrollment (KRS/KHS). (*Penjadwalan mata kuliah dan pengelolaan KRS/KHS.*)
- 💰 **Portal Keuangan:** Tuition fee tracking, invoice generation, and payment validation. (*Manajemen pembayaran UKT/SPP dan laporan keuangan.*)
- 🛡️ **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Strict permission barriers ensuring data integrity per role. (*Pengamanan akses ketat sesuai peran.*)
