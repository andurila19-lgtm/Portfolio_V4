## 📋 Project Overview (Ringkasan Proyek)

**Mommy Skincare** is an elegant product catalog and service platform dedicated to skincare and beauty care solutions tailored for mothers and women.

*Mommy Skincare adalah platform katalog produk dan layanan perawatan kulit (skincare) yang aman, elegan, dan responsif, dirancang khusus untuk memenuhi kebutuhan kecantikan dan perawatan ibu serta wanita.*

## 🔍 Features (Fitur Utama)

- 🧴 **Product Catalog:** Interactive skincare catalog categorized by skin concerns and treatment types. (*Katalog produk skincare terstruktur berdasarkan tipe kulit dan perawatan.*)
- 🌸 **Modern Beauty Aesthetic:** Soft color palette and clean typography for a relaxing user experience. (*Desain estetis dengan palet warna lembut dan tipografi bersih.*)
- 📲 **Seamless Consultation & Inquiry:** Direct integration for customer inquiries and treatment bookings. (*Integrasi konsultasi dan pemesanan layanan secara langsung.*)
- ⚡ **Mobile First & Responsive:** Optimized load speed and mobile experience. (*Performa cepat dan nyaman diakses melalui perangkat seluler.*)
