## 📋 Project Overview (Ringkasan Proyek)

**Kasir Modern (KasirPro)** is a comprehensive Point of Sale (POS) application designed to streamline business operations, inventory tracking, and sales analytics for retail and service businesses.

*Kasir Modern (KasirPro) adalah aplikasi Point of Sale (POS) komprehensif yang dirancang untuk mempermudah operasional bisnis, pemantauan stok inventaris, serta analisis laporan penjualan secara real-time.*

## 🔍 Features (Fitur Utama)

- 🛒 **Point of Sale (POS):** Fast, responsive, and intuitive cashier transaction interface. (*Transaksi kasir cepat dan mudah digunakan.*)
- 📦 **Inventory Management:** Product cataloging, stock level monitoring, and automatic updates. (*Manajemen produk dan stok otomatis.*)
- 📊 **Sales Reports & Analytics:** Real-time revenue insights, top-selling items, and transaction history. (*Laporan pendapatan dan riwayat transaksi.*)
- 🔐 **Admin Suite & Auth:** Secure access management for store managers and cashiers. (*Manajemen akses aman untuk admin dan kasir.*)
- 📱 **Fully Responsive:** Seamlessly accessible across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. (*Tampilan optimal di semua perangkat.*)
