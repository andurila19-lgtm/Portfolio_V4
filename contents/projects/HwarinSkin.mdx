## 📋 Project Overview (Ringkasan Proyek)

**Hwarin Skin** is a local skincare brand based in Madiun, Indonesia. The brand offers high-quality skincare products tailored for Indonesian skin profiles. To grow their business, transition from manual social-media sales, and build customer trust, they needed a comprehensive digital storefront.

*Hwarin Skin adalah merek skincare lokal yang berbasis di Madiun, Indonesia. Merek ini menawarkan produk perawatan kulit berkualitas tinggi. Untuk mengembangkan bisnis, bertransisi dari penjualan media sosial manual, dan membangun kepercayaan pelanggan, mereka membutuhkan etalase digital yang komprehensif.*

---

## 🔍 The Challenge (Permasalahan)

Before this project, the brand operated entirely through direct messages on Instagram and WhatsApp. This manual approach created several inefficiencies:
- **Order Overhead:** Managing product inquiries and order details manually was highly error-prone and time-consuming.
- **Limited Reach:** Operating without a central website limited search engine discoverability and credibility among new customers.
- **Inventory Discrepancies:** Out-of-stock items were frequently ordered due to the lack of real-time catalog updates.

*Sebelum proyek ini berjalan, operasional penjualan dilakukan sepenuhnya melalui pesan langsung di Instagram dan WhatsApp. Pendekatan manual ini menimbulkan beberapa masalah:*
- *Beban Kerja Pemesanan: Mengelola pertanyaan produk dan detail pesanan secara manual sangat rawan kesalahan dan memakan banyak waktu.*
- *Jangkauan Terbatas: Berjalan tanpa situs web pusat membatasi visibilitas di mesin pencari dan kredibilitas di mata pelanggan baru.*
- *Ketidakcocokan Stok: Barang yang sudah habis sering kali tetap dipesan karena tidak adanya pembaruan katalog real-time.*

---

## 💡 The Solution (Solusi)

To address these challenges, I designed and built a fast, responsive catalog and e-commerce platform that serves as a single source of truth for their products.
- **Dynamic Catalog:** Customers can browse products, view details, and check real-time availability.
- **Direct Checkout & Inquiry:** Integrated a direct order-routing system that calculates purchase totals and redirects customers to pre-filled WhatsApp checkouts.
- **Premium User Interface:** Utilized clean layouts, soft color tones matching beauty/cosmetics branding guidelines, and smooth transitions to establish a professional brand image.

*Untuk mengatasi masalah tersebut, saya merancang dan membangun platform katalog serta e-commerce yang responsif dan cepat sebagai satu-satunya acuan data produk.*
- *Katalog Dinamis: Pelanggan dapat menjelajahi produk, melihat detail, dan memeriksa ketersediaan stok secara real-time.*
- *Alur Checkout & Inquiry: Mengintegrasikan sistem rute pesanan langsung yang menghitung total pembelian dan mengarahkan pelanggan ke templat checkout WhatsApp.*
- *Antarmuka Premium: Menggunakan tata letak bersih, warna lembut yang sesuai dengan identitas produk kosmetik, dan transisi halus untuk membentuk citra merek yang profesional.*

## 👤 My Role (Peran Saya)

As the **Lead Frontend Architect & Product Designer**, my responsibilities included:
- Designing the responsive visual layout and user flow.
- Setting up the database schema for products, categories, and inventory.
- Building the frontend catalog using React/Next.js and styling it with Tailwind CSS.
- Optimizing web vitals (performance, accessibility, and SEO) to ensure high visibility and fast load speeds on mobile networks.

*Sebagai Lead Frontend Architect & Product Designer, tanggung jawab saya meliputi:*
- *Merancang tata letak visual yang responsif dan alur perjalanan pengguna.*
- *Menyusun skema basis data untuk produk, kategori, dan inventaris.*
- *Membangun frontend katalog menggunakan React/Next.js dan Tailwind CSS.*
- *Mengoptimalkan web vitals (performa, aksesibilitas, dan SEO) agar performa tetap cepat di jaringan seluler.*

## ⚙️ Tech Stack (Teknologi)

- ⚛️ **Next.js 14** (App Router)
- 🔰 **TypeScript** for type safety
- 💠 **Tailwind CSS** for responsive styling
- 🗄️ **Supabase** for database management and image hosting
- ⚡ **SWR** for client-side fetching and revalidation
